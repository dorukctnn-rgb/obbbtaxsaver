FAQ

What is OBBB Saver?

A record-keeping app for the 4 new OBBB Act tax deductions: tips, overtime, car loan interest, and senior deduction.

Is this a tax filing app?

No. It helps you organize records for your tax professional or Schedule 1-A filing.

Is my data safe?

Yes. 100% local on your device. No servers, no collection.

How to cancel subscription?

iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > OBBB Saver > Cancel.

How to export?

Savings tab > Export button (Pro feature).
